Privacy

Wherever helps people in the same room pick a restaurant. It does not require an account.

Room information

Wherever stores room settings, display names, votes, and picks to run the room and let you reconnect. Rooms expire after seven days. Anyone with a room invite can join, so share invites with the people you want in the room.

Individual ballots are not shown to other participants. People in small rooms may infer votes from the result. Hosts can optionally show who has finished voting.

Location and restaurant data

If you allow device location or search for an area, Wherever uses it to find nearby restaurants. Live search sends the search area or location query to Google. Google handles that information under its Privacy Policy.

Browser storage and usage

Your browser can remember your name and room identity for the tab, your preferences, and public app assets. Wherever records aggregate usage events such as page category and round completion. The app uses request IP addresses to limit abuse.
